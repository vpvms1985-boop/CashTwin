📖 Excel & CSV Upload Guide

Required structure, multi-sheet workbook schemas, and field definitions.

CashTwin models your cash flow twin by analyzing 4 core financial record categories. You can upload an Excel workbook (.xlsx) containing separate tabs/sheets or combined CSV files (.csv).

1. Invoices (Accounts Receivable) Sheet: Invoices
• Invoice ID: Unique alphanumeric code (e.g. INV-2026-001)
• Customer Name: Counterparty entity name
• Amount: Gross receivable amount in INR (₹)
• Due Date: Contractual payment due date (YYYY-MM-DD)
• Expected Payment Date: Estimated cash realization date
• Payment Status: Pending / Paid / Overdue
• Concentration Risk: High / Medium / Low exposure share
2. Expenses (Accounts Payable & Utilities) Sheet: Expenses
• Expense ID: Unique ID (e.g. EXP-2026-101)
• Payee / Vendor: Supplier or expense entity
• Expense Category: Raw Materials, Payroll, Utilities, Logistics
• Amount: Payable amount in INR (₹)
• Due Date: Payment cut-off date (YYYY-MM-DD)
• Recurring Status: Upcoming / Recurring / One-time
3. Payment History (Counterparty Behavior) Sheet: Payment History
• Customer Name: Client identifier
• Average Payment Delay: Historic average days past term
• Payment Behaviour: Stable / Moderate Delay / Volatile
• Risk Rating: Behavioral risk category (High / Med / Low)
4. Bank Transactions (Cash Position) Sheet: Bank Transactions
• Transaction Date: Clearing timestamp (YYYY-MM-DD)
• Description: Transaction narrative
• Credit (INR): Cash deposit inflow
• Debit (INR): Outflow settlement amount
• Balance (INR): Net account ledger balance
